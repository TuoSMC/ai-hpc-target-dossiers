1. Bottom line

Anduril Industries, Inc. is a privately held Delaware defense-technology manufacturer headquartered at 1400 Anduril, Costa Mesa, CA 92626-1548, running AS393319 across a six-metro colocation network and roughly 1.5 million SF of leased Orange County real estate plus a 500-acre, 5M SF-at-build-out megafactory at Ashville, Ohio that entered production in March 2026 (ARIN RDAP AS393319; PeeringDB netfac net_id 37589; OCBJ; Anduril Arsenal-1). It raised $5B at a $61B post-money valuation on 2026-05-13 on $2.2B of 2025 revenue (TechCrunch, 2026-05-13). Commercially this is a platform-conversion account with no identifiable server incumbent — a keyword sweep of the full text of all 2,216 live requisitions on Anduril's own Greenhouse board API returns zero mentions of Supermicro, Super Micro, Dell EMC/Dell Technologies, Lenovo or DGX, while Pure Storage and VAST Data are named outright in the storage layer — proof that Anduril does specify vendors when it has chosen one, and that it has not chosen a server vendor. The single strongest commercial signal is req 5173215007, "Infrastructure Deployment Administrator" (Costa Mesa, published 2026-06-30, $129,000–$171,000), whose stated job is "hardware receipt, asset-tagging, inventory management, staging, pre-configuration, coordinating shipments, and installations" for "new site builds" — a standing, funded, repeatable server-purchase-and-deploy motion tied to real-estate expansion. Two things can kill the deal. First, Anduril is partly a competitor: it designs and manufactures the Voyager compute line "at industrial volume", sells a "modular data center", and ships Menace-I ruggedized mobile data centers with AWS Outposts inside (DefenseScoop, 2026-07-01) — a rugged-edge pitch walks straight into their own P&L. Second, federal flow-downs move the qualification burden to the first meeting, not the third: Anduril's own req 5127590007 names CMMC, JSIG, ICD 503 CSEIG, SSECG, NIST SP 800-160 verbatim, and Section 889 / DFARS 252.204-7012 exposure will be diligenced on any server destined for a program. Bring supply-chain provenance on day one — and bring capex terms, not financing, because the UCC record shows they finance copiers and machine tools and never compute.
