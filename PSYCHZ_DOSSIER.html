1. Bottom line

Psychz Networks is the operating brand of Profuse Solutions, Inc., a founder-controlled California corporation that runs a self-operated ~15,000 sq ft Los Angeles datacenter at 700 Wilshire Blvd plus leased space across 16–17 interconnection facilities worldwide, selling bare-metal dedicated servers, colocation, DDoS mitigation, IP transit, CDN and — newly — quote-only NVIDIA GPU servers on AS40676. The commercial verdict is incumbent defence + refresh + GPU line extension: Supermicro is a strong circumstantial read on the existing fleet, their own reference architecture shows a live 3rd/4th/5th Gen Xeon Scalable refresh cycle underway, and they are marketing H100/H200/B200/B300 capacity with no configurator, no published price and no named platform vendor — the signature of a provider sourcing GPU nodes deal-by-deal rather than off a standing supply agreement. 10.1 Rent-derived ceiling

Method. Ceiling = monthly price × contribution margin × payback months, where contribution margin is the share of gross rental revenue left after operating cost. All ceilings below are computed at a 55% contribution margin (a 45% operating-cost deduction), the midpoint of the band stated next. Ceilings are rounded to the nearest dollar.

READ THIS BEFORE THE TABLE — the operating-cost deduction is an ASSUMPTION, not a researched fact. This is the single most important caveat in this section. Psychz publishes no cost data, no margin data and no financials — the entity is private and files no public accounts. The band below is an industry-norm judgement applied by the analyst, and every ceiling figure moves proportionally with it. At a 35% deduction the ceilings rise by ~18%; at a 55% deduction they fall by ~18%. Treat the ceilings as an order-of-magnitude envelope, not a computed fact.

  • ASSUMPTION — operating cost = 35% to 55% of gross monthly rental revenue, midpoint 45%, covering power, bandwidth, rack/floor, DDoS mitigation, IPv4, remote hands, provisioning labour, support, and an allowance for churn and rack vacancy. What the gap means for pitching an integrated platform.

First, and bluntly: on CPU, self-assembly wins and it is not close. A used-market build recovers its cost in 2.4 to 7 months against this operator's own rack rates. An integrated new-build platform is not competing for a $15,299 budget on the Platinum 8270 SKU — it is competing against a $1,515 pile of parts that already works. Any pitch that opens with new-versus-new price-per-core will lose. Fifteen years of catalogue evidence — Atom D525 from 2010 and Xeon E3-1230 v2 from 2012 still earning revenue in 2026 — says this buyer has already made this calculation many times and is entirely comfortable with it.

Second, the arithmetic that used to make self-assembly unbeatable is inverting, and memory is the reason. In the Silver 4214 build, memory is 72–76% of the priced BOM; in the Platinum 8270 build it is 52%. The CPUs — 52 Cascade Lake cores — cost less than half of what the RAM costs. And DDR4 ECC is in a documented 2026 supply crisis, quoted at $400–$1,000+ per new module against the $198 used here, with refurbished supply tightening. Every one of these roll-ups is a snapshot of a cost base that is actively rising underneath them. Their entire fleet economics depend on cheap secondhand DDR4 that is becoming scarce. That is the wedge, and it is a genuine one rather than a talking point: a DDR5 platform bought new is measured against DDR4 that may cost more than DDR5 within the refresh window. Model that comparison explicitly, using their own $298 and $1,159 SKUs as the revenue side.

Third, and most important, there is exactly one part of their business where self-assembly is structurally impossible. They already sell H100 80GB SXM. SXM5 modules are not sold loose — they ship only through NVIDIA-certified OEM partners as complete HGX systems, at $250,000–$320,000 for an 8-GPU node ($31,000–$40,000 per GPU). No amount of eBay sourcing skill substitutes for a certified HGX baseboard. Note what their own catalogue then says: the highest-revenue SKU they publish supports about $15,300 of hardware at a 24-month payback, which is under half the cost of a single H100 SXM module. Their entire priced CPU catalogue cannot fund one GPU. That is why the GPU line is quote-only, unpriced and unstocked while every CPU SKU carries a price and an in_stock flag — GPUs are bought against a signed customer order, not held as inventory.

So the pitch is not components and it is not price-per-core. It is the integrated HGX/SXM platform, sold into a buyer who is already a channel buyer for that exact class of hardware, who has no self-build alternative on it, and whose binding constraint is working-capital timing between customer signature and customer cash rather than unit price. Bring delivery certainty, staged delivery, and a credit or PO structure, and the deal turns on terms rather than discount. Keep the H100 94GB NVL variant in view as the control case — that one is PCIe and they can and will self-integrate it, which is precisely why it should not be the centre of the proposal.

14. Supermicro sales angle

Classification: high-probability existing / quasi-existing account → incumbent defence + generational refresh + GPU line extension

Not a greenfield play, not a displacement play, and not a platform conversion — but also not an account you may address as a confirmed customer. Supermicro here is graded circumstantial, and the correct move is to convert the inference into a question rather than assert it.

The honest wedge, in three parts:

10. 成本天花板

10.1 由租金推導的天花板

方法。 天花板 = 月租價 × 貢獻毛利率 × 回收月數，其中貢獻毛利率是租金總收入扣除營運成本後的比例。以下所有天花板均以 55% 貢獻毛利率（即扣除 45% 營運成本）計算，即下列區間的中點。天花板四捨五入至整數美元。

看表之前請先讀這段——營運成本扣除比例是「假設」，不是查證所得的事實。 這是本節最重要的但書。Psychz 不公布任何成本、毛利或財務資料——該實體為私人公司，不提交公開財報。以下區間是分析者採用的產業常規判斷，每一個天花板數字都會隨之等比移動。扣除率為 35% 時天花板上升約 18%；為 55% 時下降約 18%。請把這些天花板視為量級包絡，而非計算出的事實。

第一，直說：在 CPU 上，自組勝出，而且差距不小。 以這家業者自己的機櫃租金計，二手市場自組建置的回收期是 2.4 到 7 個月。整合式全新平台在 Platinum 8270 這個 SKU 上不是在爭取 $15,299 的預算——它是在跟一堆已經能跑、成本 $1,515 的零件競爭。任何以「全新 vs. 全新、每核心價格」開場的推銷都會輸。十五年的型錄證據——2010 年的 Atom D525 與 2012 年的 Xeon E3-1230 v2 到 2026 年還在賺錢——說明這個買家早已把這筆帳算過很多次，而且完全安於此。

第二，過去讓自組無可匹敵的算術正在反轉，原因是記憶體。 在 Silver 4214 建置中，記憶體占已詢價 BOM 的 72–76%；在 Platinum 8270 建置中占 52%。CPU——52 顆 Cascade Lake 核心——成本還不到 RAM 的一半。而 DDR4 ECC 正處於已被記錄的 2026 年供給危機，全新報價每條 $400–$1,000+，對照這裡採用的 $198，且整新供給持續吃緊。上面每一份彙總，都是一個正在他們腳下升高的成本基礎的快照。 他們整個機隊的經濟性，建立在正在變稀缺的便宜二手 DDR4 上。這就是楔子，而且是真實的楔子、不是話術： 全新買進的 DDR5 平台，對照的是在換機窗口內可能比 DDR5 還貴的 DDR4。請把這個比較明確建模，收入面就用他們自己的 $298 與 $1,159 SKU。

第三，也是最重要的：他們的業務中有恰好一塊，自組在結構上不可能。 他們已經在賣 H100 80GB SXM。SXM5 模組不以散件銷售——只能透過 NVIDIA 認證 OEM 夥伴以完整 HGX 系統出貨，8 卡節點 $250,000–$320,000（每卡 $31,000–$40,000）。再高明的 eBay 採購功力也代替不了一塊認證 HGX 基板。再看他們自家型錄怎麼說：他們公布的最高收入 SKU，在 24 個月回收期下只支撐約 $15,300 的硬體，不到單一顆 H100 SXM 模組價格的一半。他們整份標價 CPU 型錄，連一顆 GPU 都養不起。 這正是 GPU 產品線報價制、無價格、無庫存，而每個 CPU SKU 都有價格與 in_stock 旗標的原因——GPU 是對著已簽的客戶訂單去買的，不是備成庫存的。

所以這個提案不是零組件，也不是每核心價格，而是整合式 HGX/SXM 平台——賣給一個在該類硬體上已經是通路買家、沒有自組替代方案、且真正的限制是客戶簽約到客戶付款之間的營運資金時點而非單價的買家。把交期確定性、分批交付、以及信用或 PO 架構帶進去，這筆案子就會在條件（terms）上而非折扣上決勝。同時把 H100 94GB NVL 版本放在視野內當對照組——那一款是 PCIe，他們能自行整合、也一定會自行整合，正因如此，它不該是提案的核心。
