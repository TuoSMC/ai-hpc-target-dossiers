9. Supermicro Sales Angle

Honest read of the buying chain (all from verified facts above):

  1. Galaxy is NOT the server buyer for the contracted 800 MW. Galaxy is the landlord (powered shell + fit-out); tenant CoreWeave deploys its own IT/GPU equipment (10-K FY2025; Galaxy PR 2026-07-06). GPU/rack/OEM selection at Helios is undisclosed (GAP) and sits with CoreWeave, not Galaxy.
  2. The near-term server TAM at Helios is CoreWeave's procurement. Phase II (260 MW critical IT) data halls deliver from H1 2027 and Phase III (133 MW) completes by 2028 (PR 2026-07-06; 10-K) — meaning CoreWeave's fill-out purchasing for 393 MW of incremental critical IT load is still ahead. Approach path is the CoreWeave account, not Galaxy. Before any approach, check the internal lead-registration / taken-blocked AI-DC account map — CoreWeave may already be claimed by another team.
  3. The Galaxy-side wedge is the uncontracted ~830 MW. Galaxy has ~1.63 GW approved gross (ERCOT +830 MW in Q1 2026) and is in discussions with potential tenants for the ~830 MW beyond CoreWeave (Q1 2026 PR; 10-K), plus a stated multi-campus, multi-tenant, multi-GW ambition (PR 2026-07-06). Every future Helios tenant is a future server buyer. Cultivating Galaxy's leasing/BD pipeline (public voices: Novogratz, Ferraro — no Head of Data Centers exists, GAP) gives Supermicro early sight of the next tenant before the tenant's own vendor bake-off starts.
  4. Design-density fit. Helios markets 130–140 kW rack density targets (galaxy.com data-centers page — marketing claim, single-source). That density class implies liquid-cooled, high-density racks — squarely Supermicro DLC territory for whichever tenant fills the next halls.
  5. Timing triggers to watch: Q2 2026 earnings call 2026-08-05 (first full Helios rent quarter — likely venue for new tenant/leasing news); Phase II notes close ~2026-07-28 ($3.507B, 8-K 2026-07-22) confirms Phase II construction funding; any LOI announcement on the residual ~830 MW (currently GAP).
  6. What Galaxy itself might buy: nothing in the verified material shows Galaxy purchasing servers/GPUs at scale — GAP. Its own compute needs (trading infrastructure, GK8 custody tech) are not quantified in any source reviewed. Do not pitch Galaxy as a GPU-server volume buyer; pitch the relationship for tenant-pipeline intelligence and future-phase/tenant referral.

Entity to approach: CoreWeave (server purchases for Phases II–III, subject to account-claim check) and Galaxy corporate/BD (tenant pipeline on ~830 MW + future campuses). Wedge: powered-shell adjacency — be present before the next tenant signs.

9. Supermicro 銷售切入點

採購鏈的誠實解讀（全數依上文查證事實）：

  1. 就已簽約的 800 MW 而言，Galaxy 不是伺服器買方。 Galaxy 是房東（powered shell + 機電建置）；租戶 CoreWeave 自行部署自有 IT/GPU 設備（10-K FY2025；Galaxy 新聞稿 2026-07-06）。Helios 之 GPU/機櫃/OEM 選擇未揭露（GAP），決定權在 CoreWeave 而非 Galaxy。向 Galaxy 推銷 CoreWeave 機房用伺服器是誤讀這個客戶。
  2. Helios 近期的伺服器商機在 CoreWeave 的採購。 Phase II（260 MW 關鍵 IT）資料機房自 2027 上半年起交付，Phase III（133 MW）於 2028 年前完成（新聞稿 2026-07-06；10-K）— 即 CoreWeave 針對 393 MW 增量關鍵 IT 負載的填充採購仍在前方。接觸路徑是 CoreWeave 客戶線，不是 Galaxy。接觸前務必先查內部 lead registration / AI-DC 已占用帳戶地圖 — CoreWeave 可能已由其他團隊認列。
  3. Galaxy 端的楔子是未簽約的約 830 MW。 Galaxy 已核准毛電力約 1.63 GW（ERCOT 於 Q1 2026 加核 +830 MW），CoreWeave 之外的約 830 MW 正與潛在租戶洽談（Q1 2026 新聞稿；10-K），並已表明多園區、多租戶、多 GW 的企圖（新聞稿 2026-07-06）。每一個未來的 Helios 租戶都是未來的伺服器買方。經營 Galaxy 的招租/BD 管道（對外發言人：Novogratz、Ferraro — 無資料中心負責人職稱，GAP）能讓 Supermicro 在下一個租戶啟動供應商評選前先行卡位。
  4. 設計密度契合。 Helios 行銷 130–140 kW 機櫃密度目標（galaxy.com 資料中心頁面 — 行銷宣稱，單一來源）。此密度等級意味著液冷高密度機櫃 — 正是 Supermicro DLC 的主場，適用於填充後續機房的任何租戶。
  5. 應追蹤的時點觸發： Q2 2026 法說會 2026-08-05（Helios 首個完整收租季度 — 新租戶/招租消息的可能發布場合）；Phase II 債券約 2026-07-28 交割（$3.507B，8-K 2026-07-22）確認 Phase II 工程資金到位；殘餘約 830 MW 的任何 LOI 公告（目前為 GAP）。
  6. Galaxy 自身可能採購什麼： 查證資料中無 Galaxy 大規模採購伺服器/GPU 的證據 — GAP。其自身運算需求（交易基礎設施、GK8 託管技術）在所查來源中未量化。勿將 Galaxy 定位為 GPU 伺服器量體買家；應以租戶管道情報與後續期別/租戶引介為經營目的。

應接觸的實體： CoreWeave（Phase II–III 伺服器採購，須先確認帳戶認列狀態）與 Galaxy 公司/BD（約 830 MW 招租管道 + 未來園區）。楔子： powered-shell 鄰接策略 — 在下一個租戶簽約之前先到場。
