1. Bottom line

Input Output Flood LLC, trading as IOFLOOD, is a founder-owned, single-decider dedicated-server and bare-metal-cloud host that runs AS53755 out of a 10,718 sq ft warehouse it owns outright at 1347–1349 E University Dr, Tempe, Arizona, bought for $1,135,000 on 2018-12-01 (Maricopa County Assessor, APNs 132-59-049 / 132-59-048; deed #20180954203). It self-publishes "over 1,000 servers rented to customers from our self-owned datacenter in Tempe, AZ" (VTunnel retrospective, published 2023-11-28) and 200 kW critical IT load at launch, design-expandable to 1 MW with 2N redundancy (Tempe DC announcement, 2021-10-05) — two independently published numbers that, taken together at a realistic mixed-workload ~200 W per server, corroborate each other exactly.

Commercially this is an incumbent-retention account with a live displacement risk, not a greenfield conversion. Supermicro is a confirmed incumbent — IOFlood still says so today on two live pages: "We use Supermicro or Dell server grade motherboards…" (whyus.php) and "We use Supermicro and Dell enterprise grade servers…" (cpanel-dedicated-servers.php), corroborated independently by a 2013 customer attestation on WebHostingTalk. But on the flagship product page the Supermicro and Dell names were deleted between 2023-12-02 and 2024-01-01 — exactly as the company pivoted to AMD Ryzen and EPYC (phoenix-dedicated-servers.php, Wayback-bracketed). The AMD-era page describes precisely the spec Supermicro sells — "server grade motherboards with ECC RAM and IPMI remote management" (ryzen-dedicated-servers.php) — and pointedly declines to name who supplies it.

So the entire account reduces to one question: did we lose the AM5 / EPYC-4004 socket in late 2023, or did we keep it and stop getting the credit? That question is answerable in one email, and the timing is unusually good: every one of the 30 EPYC SKUs in the live catalogue is at zero stock, Broadwell inventory is being actively cleared under a live campaign code (July2026-95v4), and only 55 sellable units remain across 78 SKUs. The restock decision is on the founder's desk this quarter.

The things that can kill it: he buys hyperscaler-custom decommissioned parts (EPYC 7V13, 7B13) and publishes his own quarterly used-part price graphs (Used server parts Q2/2022, 2022-05-19), so he prices any new quote against the secondary market in his head within seconds; and there is not one equipment-finance UCC filing in his entire history — servers are bought with cash from operations, so financing is not a lever.

10. 成本天花板

一台伺服器對 IOFLOOD 值多少錢，以及同一台機器要花多少錢組起來。本節只回答一個問題：在多少硬體成本以內，一個 IOFLOOD SKU 仍然回本？

10.1 假設——請先讀這一段；這些是假設，不是查證發現

  • 回本慣例：以 9–12 個月毛營收回收硬體成本。 非託管型專用伺服器業者通常以此為目標，因為之後那台機器還會再租 4–7 年。這是本檔套用的產業慣例，不是 IOFLOOD 公布的數字。 因此以下每一個天花板都是模型推估值。
  • colo 租金假設在此不適用，而這是對原始紀錄的框架修正。 原始 tier 紀錄假設的是由租金推導的天花板。IOFlood 自有建物，無任何房東。 其固定成本底線是 $1,135,000 貸款的還本付息，加上以 15% 估價比率對合計 $1,889,900 全額現值課徵的財產稅（核定 LPV $10,210 ＋ $100,609）。成本結構遠比 colo 租戶輕——這正是他們能把雙路 E5 機器掛在 $89/月、還能用現金買硬體的原因。不要對他們談以 colo 租金為前提的 TCO 節省。
  • 機隊基準：自行公布的 1,000 台下限（6.1 節）。那是 2023 年底的下限，不是現行普查值。
  • 未納入的項目： 資金成本、業務／支援／授權間接費用、生命週期末端的殘值（對二手 Xeon 與儲存機殼而言相當可觀，且會提高有效天花板），以及任何量產採購議價力。
