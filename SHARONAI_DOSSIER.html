  4. This is not practically a T1 USA hardware opportunity. The 10-Q self-describes the company as "an Australian neocloud operator." 1. Bottom line

Sharon AI is a Nasdaq-listed (SHAZ) Australian neocloud operator wearing a Delaware corporate shell, and the honest verdict for this team is that it should be escalated to APAC/Australia coverage rather than registered as a Texas lead. The registrable US entity is SharonAI Holdings Inc., incorporated in Delaware with a principal office at 745 Fifth Avenue, New York; the entity that actually buys hardware is SharonAI Pty Ltd, which signs the Lenovo SOW and every executive employment agreement, and the entire fleet lives at NEXTDC M3 Melbourne and NEXTDC S3 Sydney, plus GreenSquareDC Sydney and an undisclosed New Zealand facility (Form 10-Q; Form S-1, 2026-07-31). The scale is real and enormous on paper — ~$4 billion of procurement commitment under a six-year NVIDIA collaboration signed 2026-06-12, up to 40,000 Grace Blackwell GB300 GPUs, 62,000+ GPUs planned by mid-2027, ~132 MW of AI Factory capacity of which 116 MW is contracted — but installed capacity today is roughly 2,000 GPUs and trailing half-year revenue is $2,225,396 against $2.27 bn of announced contract value.

Commercially this is a hard displacement, not greenfield, and four separate parties have a commercial interest in keeping the current OEM mix intact. Lenovo is confirmed on the compute platform: the company's own published node spec lists "24 x 32GB TruDDR5 4800MHz RAM" — TruDDR5 is Lenovo's proprietary memory brand — and the 10-Q discloses $33,094 thousand of Equipment-as-a-Service / Device-as-a-Service down payments to Lenovo Global Financial Services (Australia & New Zealand) Pty Limited plus a 60-month managed-infrastructure Statement of Work with ~50% paid upfront (sharonai.com/ai-cloud/; Form 10-Q). World Wide Technology (WWT Australia Pty Ltd) holds $256,186 thousand — $256.2 million — of equipment prepayments as integrator of record. Cisco is locked in structurally, not merely commercially: Digital Alpha Advisors, which holds an exclusive technology partnership with Cisco, invested up to US$200M and is a shareholder, and the flagship B300 cluster is branded "Australia's first Cisco Secure AI Factory." NVIDIA controls the OEM shortlist through NCP status, DSX reference designs and a revenue-share plus credit-support structure.

Supermicro's evidence grade in this account is circumstantial and must not be upgraded in conversation. Supermicro appears exactly once across the 10-Q, the DEF 14A, three 8-Ks and the 4.6 MB S-1, and it is not as a purchasing relationship — it is one name in a list of OEMs that NVIDIA Cloud Partner status gives the company access to: "OEM Relationships: Facilitated engagement with OEMs such as Cisco, Lenovo, Dell, and Super Micro to support server procurement and integration" (Form S-1). No purchase order, no prepayment, no commitment, no named agreement, no Supermicro hardware in any published node spec. Do not tell anyone internally that Supermicro is an incumbent here.

The one genuine opening is timing and concentration risk. A company consuming hardware as a financed service while carrying $256.2M prepaid to a single integrator, against ~$4 bn of committed procurement, on a 70-person team, has concentrated supply-chain and prepayment exposure sitting on a balance sheet that just raised $900M. A brand-new CFO, Anuj Goel, starts 2026-08-24 — two weeks after this research date — and will own that risk. The credible pitch is supply-base diversification and delivery certainty for the April–August 2027 five-phase NEXTDC ramp, not price and not features. And under Rule 8 this must not be registered as an end-user deal: hardware flows through WWT Australia (integrator), Lenovo Global Financial Services ANZ (financier) and Dicker Data Limited (distributor), so the channel position has to be cleared before any registration attempt.

Four separate parties therefore have a commercial interest in keeping the current OEM mix intact.

The one genuine opening

The company is consuming hardware as a financed service while carrying $256.2M prepaid to a single integrator, against ~$4 bn of committed procurement and a 70-person team. That is concentrated supply-chain and prepayment risk sitting on a balance sheet that just raised $900M. A new CFO, Anuj Goel, starts 2026-08-24 and will own that risk.

The credible pitch is supply-base diversification and delivery certainty for the April–August 2027 five-phase NEXTDC ramp — not price, and not features. Supermicro's realistic claim is on the ~15–20% of node value that is not the GPU cards, plus lead-time and rack-integration capacity for GB300 NVL72 at a scale where Lenovo and Cisco allocation may be constrained.

Sharon AI 是一家在那斯達克掛牌（SHAZ）、實質上是澳洲 neocloud 業者、外面套著德拉瓦州公司殼的公司；對本組而言，誠實的判斷是應該把它轉交 APAC／澳洲轄區承接，而不是登錄為德州 lead。可註冊的美國法人是 SharonAI Holdings Inc.，設立於德拉瓦州、主要營業處所在紐約 745 Fifth Avenue；真正買硬體的法人是 SharonAI Pty Ltd，Lenovo 的 SOW 與所有高階主管聘僱合約都由它簽署，而整支機隊落在 NEXTDC M3（墨爾本）與 NEXTDC S3（雪梨），加上 GreenSquareDC 雪梨與一處未揭露的紐西蘭設施（Form 10-Q；Form S-1，2026-07-31）。帳面上的規模確實巨大——2026-06-12 簽訂的六年期 NVIDIA 合作案帶來 約 40 億美元的採購承諾、最高 40,000 顆 Grace Blackwell GB300 GPU、2027 年中前規劃部署超過 62,000 顆 GPU、AI Factory 容量約 132 MW（其中 116 MW 已與終端客戶簽約）——但今天實際安裝的約只有 2,000 顆 GPU，且上半年營收僅 $2,225,396，相對於已公告的 22.7 億美元合約總值。

  17. 重跑時的工具註記 —— 數個 MCP 連接器（ZoomInfo、carta、figma、atlassian、spglobal、adobe）需 OAuth 授權，在本次非互動式工作階段中無法使用。若透過 claude.ai 連接器設定授權 ZoomInfo，重跑時很可能可以補上 LinkedIn 與美國具名員工這兩塊缺口。另請注意：WebFetch 對 sec.gov 回傳 HTTP 403；以符合 SEC 規範之 User-Agent 發出 curl 請求則可通，本檔所有申報文件都是循此取得。
