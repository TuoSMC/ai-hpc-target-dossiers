Enzu Inc is a privately held, founder-run bare-metal hosting, colocation, GPU-rental and hardware-procurement group that trades under at least four legal entities and three consumer-facing brands — Enzu, Inc. (Nevada, incorporated 2005-03-25), Enzu LLC (Wyoming, foreign-qualified in Colorado), BudgetVM LLC (Wyoming) and Dash Networks Inc. Commercially this is not a hunt — it is a defend-and-expand account, and the position being defended is already partially lost. The single strongest piece of evidence sits on the customer's own website today: BudgetVM's storage-server page names "Supermicro Server Hardware" with no other system vendor, while its GPU page names "Supermicro / Dell Hardware" (storage, GPU, read 2026-08-17). Supermicro is sole-named on the low-margin, long-life commodity estate; Dell has been let in exactly where the new money is. The arithmetic makes that dilution urgent rather than cosmetic: at Enzu's own published $4,699/month for a 2 × H100 NVL box, the sourced GPU-plus-CPU floor of $48,900 cannot be repaid on a 12- or 18-month hurdle at any operating-cost assumption tested, and clears 24 months only at the most favourable corner (§10) — meaning a vendor who moves their acquisition cost by 10% moves their gross margin by more than 10%, because the platform is the dominant line and the price side has been frozen for 51 months on the legacy estate. Timing is the other half: four refresh events between May 2024 and May 2025 at a ~3.4-month median interval, then 14.6 months of nothing, with B200 and B300 already advertised on enzu.com's home page and zero Blackwell SKUs anywhere in the priced catalogue — precisely the state the GPU line was in immediately before its February 2025 launch. The one thing that could kill the deal is geography and governance: the "Denver account" is a facility, not a decision centre. The principals file from Michigan, the operating entities are Wyoming LLCs, the historic corporation is Nevada, and the CRM/channel position was not verified in this pass — so under Rule 8 the distributor and CRM position must be cleared before the first call.

Three conclusions follow, and they are the wedge:

  1. Enzu is not paying street for silicon. They are buying tray/volume, secondary-market, or ex-lease. Any quote that assumes list-price comparison will be dismissed in the first minute.
  2. Enzu amortises far longer than 24 months. The 2012-era E3s still earning at an unchanged $83.88 for 51 straight months prove the house style is 8–12 year asset lives. Pitch total cost of ownership over a decade, not payback over two years.
  3. Every dollar of platform cost is existential to them. A vendor who moves their acquisition cost by 10% moves their gross margin by more than 10%, because the platform is the dominant line and the price side is frozen — they have not raised a legacy price in 51 months and have not moved a GPU price in 16.

What this means for how to pitch:

  • Do not lead with integration savings. They install everything themselves — "servers were all installed by us and only for us" — and they will beat any integrator on assembly labour. A pitch built on "we save you integration effort" loses on arithmetic.
  • Lead with cost per usable GPU-watt and GPUs per chassis. On a 1.4–1.8 kW two-GPU node, chassis density is the variable that moves both the numerator and the denominator. The conversation that wins is GPUs per chassis, not dollars per chassis.
  • Attack the power line, not the price line, on the legacy estate. A density-and-power replacement argument that collapses many 2012-era 1U boxes into fewer current-generation nodes attacks their single largest cost — colocation power — without asking them to raise a price they clearly cannot raise.
  • Expect an acquisition-cost conversation, not a discount conversation. At these ratios no rational operator funds the flagship from cash at street prices, which is consistent with the 14.6-month freeze (§9) and with the secondary-market inference (§8.4).

14. Supermicro sales angle

Classification: Named incumbent under active dilution → defend the storage line, reclaim the GPU line

This is NOT a new-logo pitch and it is NOT an uncontested incumbency. Supermicro is already named on Enzu's own public hardware specification — sole-named on the storage line, and shared with Dell on the GPU line (storage · GPU, read 2026-08-17). That asymmetry is the entire story. Supermicro owns the low-margin, long-life, commodity estate. Dell has been let in exactly where the money now is.

Three facts frame the conversation:

  1. The arithmetic is against them. At sourced street prices the flagship 2 × H100 NVL SKU cannot repay on a 12- or 18-month hurdle at any operating-cost assumption tested (§10). They are squeezed from both sides — platform cost they cannot absorb, and a price they demonstrably cannot raise (51 months unchanged on the legacy estate, 16 months unchanged on GPU).
  2. Their fastest-growing business is not renting servers at all — it is buying servers on customers' behalf. 8 × H200 SXM5 nodes with 2 × EPYC 9654, 200G InfiniBand, GRAID/Weka storage, 310 kW scaling to 1.2 MW, one client scaled from 256 to 1,000+ GPUs. Enzu explicitly sells "Hardware Planning & Procurement". Every node in that pipeline is a node Supermicro either books or watches Dell book.
  3. The vendor on those H200 pods is unknown and must be treated as unknown. An 8×H200 SXM5 + 2×EPYC 9654 + 200G IB node is a textbook Supermicro AS-8125GS-TNHR / SYS-821GE class design, but Enzu names no vendor on that page and it could equally be a Dell XE9680 or a Gigabyte platform. Do not assume it is ours.

The four wedges, in priority order

1. Reclaim the GPU line from Dell — the only wedge that matters this year. Storage says "Supermicro Server Hardware". GPU says "Supermicro / Dell Hardware". Find out when Dell was added and against which SKU. The AS-4125GS-TNRT / AS-4125GS-TNRT2 class (4U, dual EPYC 9004/9005, up to 8 double-width cards including H100 NVL, PCIe 5.0 x16 direct-attach, optional NVLink Bridge) is the exact platform their 1× and 2× H100 NVL SKUs sit on, and it is a Supermicro-native strength.

2. Blackwell productisation — the timing wedge. enzu.com advertises B200 and B300 on the home page. Zero Blackwell SKUs exist in the priced catalogue and none appear in any Wayback snapshot from February 2025 to July 2026. They are selling Blackwell into the enterprise channel today with no self-serve platform behind it. That gap is precisely the state the GPU line was in immediately before the February 2025 launch. Whoever supplies the Blackwell reference platform wins the next catalogue.

3. The frozen legacy estate — the volume wedge. E3-1230v2 (Ivy Bridge, 2012) and E3-1231v3 (Haswell, 2014) are still on the price list at $84.88 and $83.88, unchanged since at least May 2022. That is 12- and 14-year-old silicon earning revenue in 2026 — power-inefficient, out of warranty, and impossible to price up. A density-and-power replacement argument that collapses many old 1U boxes into fewer current-generation nodes attacks their single largest cost line, colocation power, without asking them to raise a price they clearly cannot raise.

4. Procurement partnership — the multiplier wedge. Enzu sells hardware procurement as a service to third parties and has a channel-native EVP of Sales who ran national channel at TierPoint. This account is worth more as a route to market than as an end customer. A co-sell motion through their TSD/VAR/MSP ecosystem monetises Dustin Young's rolodex, not just Enzu's own racks.

"Your GPU page says Supermicro and Dell, but your storage page says Supermicro only. 10.4 彙總對比——以及這個落差對訴求的意義

結論：以已具來源的街頭價計算，Enzu 的旗艦 GPU SKU 在本檔測試的「任何」營運成本假設下都無法於 12 或 18 個月回本，只有在最有利的單一角落才勉強在 24 個月打平。

以 已具來源的 GPU ＋ CPU 下限 $48,900（尚未計入任何一塊記憶體、SSD、機殼、網卡或任何一瓦電）對照 10.2 的天花板：

  • 12 個月回本：不可能。 最寬鬆的天花板 $31,010，比「最便宜的一對 H100 NVL 加一顆 CPU」還短少 37%。
  • 18 個月回本：在所有營運成本假設下皆不可能。 最佳情況 $46,510 對上 $48,900 的下限——仍然不足，而且這還沒算記憶體、SSD、機殼、網卡與電力。
  • 24 個月回本：勉強可行，且只在最有利的角落——天花板 $62,020 對上下限 $48,900，需要同時假設 45% 營運成本 且 GPU 取價落在區間底部。

主力機種的交叉查核，情況可以說更糟。 1 × AMD EPYC 9554、256 GB DDR5、2 × 960 GB SSD ＋ 2 × 3.2 TB NVMe、10 Gbps ＋ 100 TB — $999/月。以 18 個月、50% 營運成本計，整台硬體預算是 $999 × 0.5 × 18 = **$8,991**。光是 EPYC 9554 街頭價就要 $4,900–$7,900，佔整台硬體預算的 55% 至 88%。** 再加 256 GB DDR5、6.4 TB NVMe、兩顆 SATA SSD 與機殼，這台機器在任何合理攤提下都是虧的。

由此得出三個結論，也就是切入點：

  1. Enzu 不是用街頭價買矽晶。 他們買的是 tray／量販、次級市場或退租品。任何以牌價比較為前提的報價，第一分鐘就會被打掉。
  2. Enzu 的攤提遠長於 24 個月。 2012 年世代的 E3 仍以不變的 $83.88 連續 51 個月在賺錢，證明其風格是 8–12 年資產壽命。請以十年期總持有成本，而非兩年回本，來作訴求。
  3. 平台成本對他們每一塊錢都攸關存亡。 任何能把取得成本壓低 10% 的供應商，能把毛利拉高超過 10%，因為平台是最大成本項，而售價端已經凍結——老機隊 51 個月未調價，GPU 16 個月未調價。

這對訴求方式的意義：

  • 不要以「整合省下的工」開場。 他們一切自行安裝——「servers were all installed by us and only for us」——在組裝工時上會贏過任何整合商。以「我們幫你省整合工夫」為基礎的訴求，在算術上就輸了。
  • 以每瓦可用 GPU 成本與每機殼 GPU 數量開場。 在一台 1.4–1.8 kW 的雙 GPU 節點上，機殼密度是同時影響分子與分母的變數。能贏的對話是「每機殼幾張 GPU」，不是「每機殼多少錢」。
  • 對老機隊，攻電力線，不要攻價格線。 用密度與電力論述把大量 2012 年世代的 1U 主機收攏成少數當世代節點，直接打他們最大的成本項——資料中心電力——而且不需要他們去調一個明顯調不動的價格。
  • 要準備的是取得成本對話，不是折扣對話。 在這種比例下，沒有理性業者會用現金以街頭價買旗艦機——這與 14.6 個月的凍結（第 9 節）以及次級市場推論（8.4）互相一致。

14. Supermicro 銷售切入點

分類：具名既有供應商但正被稀釋 → 守住儲存線，奪回 GPU 線

這不是開發新客戶，也不是不受挑戰的既有客戶案。 Supermicro 已經被具名在 Enzu 自家的公開硬體規格上——在儲存線是唯一具名，在 GPU 線則是與 Dell 並列（storage · GPU，2026-08-17 讀取）。這個不對稱就是故事的全部。 Supermicro 擁有低毛利、長壽命、商品化的機隊；Dell 正好被放進錢在的地方。

三個事實界定了這場對話：

  1. 算術對他們不利。 以已具來源的街頭價計，旗艦 2 × H100 NVL SKU 在本檔測試的任何營運成本假設下都無法在 12 或 18 個月回本（第 10 節）。他們兩面受擠——吸收不了的平台成本，以及實證上調不動的售價（老機隊 51 個月未調，GPU 16 個月未調）。
  2. 他們成長最快的業務根本不是出租伺服器，而是替客戶買伺服器。 8 × H200 SXM5 節點、2 × EPYC 9654、200G InfiniBand、GRAID／Weka 儲存、310 kW 擴充到 1.2 MW，一個客戶由 256 張擴到 1,000+ 張 GPU。Enzu 明確銷售 「Hardware Planning & Procurement」。那條管線上的每一台節點，都是 Supermicro 拿到或看著 Dell 拿到的一台。
  3. 那些 H200 pod 的系統廠是未知，而且必須當成未知處理。 8×H200 SXM5 ＋ 2×EPYC 9654 ＋ 200G IB 的節點是教科書等級的 Supermicro AS-8125GS-TNHR／SYS-821GE 類設計，但 Enzu 在該頁未具名任何廠商，同樣可能是 Dell XE9680 或 Gigabyte 平台。不得假設是我們的。

四個切入點，依優先順序

1. 從 Dell 手上奪回 GPU 線——今年唯一真正重要的切入點。 儲存頁寫「Supermicro Server Hardware」，GPU 頁寫「Supermicro / Dell Hardware」。去問出 Dell 是什麼時候、針對哪個 SKU 被加進來的。 AS-4125GS-TNRT／AS-4125GS-TNRT2 類機種（4U、雙路 EPYC 9004/9005、最多八張雙寬卡含 H100 NVL、PCIe 5.0 x16 直連、可選 NVLink Bridge）正是其 1× 與 2× H100 NVL SKU 所在的平台，也正是 Supermicro 的原生強項。

2. Blackwell 產品化——時間點切入。 enzu.com 首頁已在宣傳 B200 與 B300。標價型錄中零個 Blackwell SKU，2025 年 2 月至 2026 年 7 月的所有快照中也都沒有。 他們今天正把 Blackwell 賣進企業通路，背後卻沒有自助平台。這個缺口正是 GPU 產品線在 2025 年 2 月上線前的狀態。誰供應 Blackwell 參考平台，誰就拿下下一版型錄。

3. 凍結的老機隊——量的切入。 E3-1230v2（Ivy Bridge，2012）與 E3-1231v3（Haswell，2014）仍以 $84.88 與 $83.88 掛在價目上，至少自 2022 年 5 月起未曾變動。那是 12 年與 14 年前的矽晶在 2026 年賺錢——耗電、過保、且無法調價。用密度與電力論述把大量老 1U 主機收攏成少數當世代節點，直接打他們最大的成本項——資料中心電力——而且不需要他們去調一個明顯調不動的價格。

4. 採購夥伴關係——乘數切入。 Enzu 把硬體採購當服務賣給第三方，且有一位在 TierPoint 掌管全國通路出身的通路型 EVP。這個案子作為「通路」的價值高於作為「終端客戶」的價值。 透過其 TSD／VAR／MSP 生態的共同銷售，變現的是 Dustin Young 的人脈，而不只是 Enzu 自己的機櫃。

「你們的 GPU 頁面寫的是 Supermicro 和 Dell，但儲存頁面只寫 Supermicro。你們首頁已經在賣 B200 和 B300，可是十八個月來沒有任何高於 H100 NVL 的東西進到價目表。當你們把 Blackwell 產品化進型錄時，它會落在這兩條線的哪一條——以及在每瓦可用 GPU 成本上要滿足什麼條件，它才會落在我們這邊？」

Rule 8 — 經銷通路注意事項（撥號前必讀）

Enzu 擁有可觀規模——自報 12,000+ 台伺服器、八個以上美國都會區、AS18978 上 168,448 個 IPv4 位址、自家網站上具名的 Supermicro 產品線——而且本次查證未確認 CRM 狀態（第 2 節）。那是缺口，不是綠燈。撥號之前必須釐清兩件事：

  1. CRM 紀錄。 對這個家族的每一個名稱字串執行 salesleads Search（Type = All）——Enzu、Enzu Inc、Enzu LLC、BudgetVM、Budget VM、BudgetVM LLC、Dash Networks、ScalableDNS——因為採購法人可能是以品牌而非公司名稱登錄。
  2. 經銷商歸屬。 Supermicro 已被具名在他們公開的規格上，代表硬體是從某處進來的——直接、經銷，或整合商。依 Rule 8，經銷商可以跨轄區，但必須事先取得核准並設定 do-not-call 旗標。 若 Enzu 已是某經銷商的既有客戶，本案就從擴張銷售轉為經銷商管理案件，開場方式、報價路徑與註冊方法全都會改變。

轄區注意事項，這不是形式問題。 CoreSite DE1 的租用關係讓本案在紙面上是 Denver, Colorado — West Coast South excl. CA = T1｜T31，但決策者由 Michigan 申報、營運法人是 Wyoming LLC、歷史法人在 Nevada。在假設本組單獨覆蓋之前，請先協調。
