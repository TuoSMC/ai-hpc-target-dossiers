1. Bottom line

Aunalytics, Inc. is a Delaware corporation operating from 460 Stull Street, South Bend, Indiana, running four ASNs and 11,264 owned IPv4 addresses across five datacenter facilities — two it says it operates itself (South Bend, Kalamazoo), one it leases in the Southfield half of this territory (123.NET DC1, carrying a 20 Gbps DET-iX port), one interconnection cage at GAP Union Station in South Bend, and one Cologix colocation in Columbus, Ohio under the NetGain subsidiary's ASN (ARIN Whois-RWS Org AUNAL · PeeringDB). It sells a live, productised GPU service — its own enterprise cloud page markets "advanced GPU and data-caching services" and "GPU and data caching for machine learning and high-volume analytics workloads" — and it names NetApp, Veeam, SentinelOne and AWS publicly while naming no server or compute vendor anywhere at all. That silence is the single most important commercial fact in this file: they will tell you who makes their storage, their backup and their endpoint agent, but not who makes their servers.

Commercially this is a platform-conversion target with a dated, publicly subsidised purchase clock, not an incumbent-defence account. They announced a $30M equipment programme in April 2024 explicitly including "a high-performance graphics processing unit to advance AI" (WNDU, 2024-04-19), committed only $8.5M to the initial phase against a $1.5M forgivable municipal loan and a ~$2.5M personal-property tax abatement on advanced computing equipment (WVPE, 2024-06-13) — leaving roughly $21.5M of a publicly announced envelope still unplaced. Between 2026-04-24 and 2026-08-03 they published four dated architecture essays under a new author cohort arguing that frontier GPU capacity is physically constrained and economically wrong for most enterprise work, and that the answer is an Intelligence Layer routing routine inference to tiers the company controls. Read correctly, that is not a commitment to buy frontier superclusters — it is the opposite, and it is a written commitment to many modest, controllable inference nodes sited next to regulated bank data. That is exactly the shape Supermicro sells best.

The thing that decides the deal is arithmetic, not architecture. Their revenue base is community banks and credit unions, and even the friendliest payback cell in §10 demands roughly $787K of incremental monthly revenue against the committed $8.5M phase alone. Their architecture demands more nodes; their P&L demands cheaper nodes. The conversation therefore lives with Kyle Urtel, the CPA CFO who holds both the Secretary and Treasurer offices, and it opens through Chad Varney, the sole ARIN administrative/technical/NOC/routing contact across all four ASNs and the only named public route into the network organisation. Two things could kill it before the first call: approaching the wrong NetGain (§3.1), and running the UCC workstream in the wrong state (§3.2).

10. Cost ceiling

What the announced equipment programme has to earn back, and at what node cost the arithmetic works. This section answers one question: how much incremental monthly revenue must the equipment generate for the programme to pay for itself — and what does that imply about $/node?

10.1 Assumptions — read these first, they are assumptions and not findings

  • The two capex inputs are CONFIRMED public facts: $30M total equipment authorisation and $8.5M committed initial phase (WNDU · WVPE).
  • THE OPERATING-COST SHARE IS AN ASSUMPTION, NOT A RESEARCHED FACT, and it is given as a range rather than a point. Operating-cost share of revenue attributable to this equipment — power, cooling, space, network, software and staff — is modelled at 55%–75%, i.e. contribution margin available for hardware payback of 25%–45%. Aunalytics disclosed no cost data, so every ceiling figure below is a modelled estimate.
  • Formula: required incremental monthly revenue = (capex ÷ payback months) ÷ contribution margin.
  • What is excluded: no cost of capital or discounting; no sales, support, licensing or overhead allocation; no residual-value credit; no allowance for the abatement's cash-tax benefit, which would improve the picture and is not modelled.
  • Payback-window choice: 12 / 18 / 24 months, presented as a commercial hurdle rate — not an asset-life constraint.

他們自己有日期、公開、具作者的論述主張：前沿 GPU 產能受物理限制、對多數企業任務在經濟上並不合適，而解方是把請求導向公司自行掌控之專用分層的 Intelligence Layer ——理由是銀行主管機關會強制執行的資料落地要求（2026-05-14 與 2026-08-03）。這是一份白紙黑字的公開承諾：要自有並自營一支由大量中等規模、可控推論節點組成的機隊，部署在他們已宣稱自營的機房（South Bend、Kalamazoo）與一座租用機房（123.NET DC1, Southfield）中受監管資料的旁邊。

現在疊上 §10。即使是最寬鬆的一格回收假設，也需要來自社區銀行與信用合作社的約每月 $787K 增額營收——而該客層不寫八位數合約。節點數量是策略；節點成本是限制。 Supermicro 的契合點正在此：密度高、可組態、緊鄰儲存、且 $/node 明顯低於一線 OEM 的中階 GPU 平台，這會直接轉換成在約 $21.5M 未落地授權額度下每一元可多出的推論分層數（§9）。
