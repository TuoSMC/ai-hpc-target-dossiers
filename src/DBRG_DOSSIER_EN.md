## 9. Supermicro sales angle

Derived honestly from the verified facts — who actually buys servers in this ecosystem:

1. **DBRG corporate does NOT buy servers.** It is an asset manager with 316 employees; no server-procurement line exists (10-K FY2025 business description; both passes).
2. **Its DC platforms (Vantage, Switch, Yondr, DataBank, Scala, AIMS, AtlasEdge) sell space/power/cooling/interconnection** — the landlord model. End tenants (cloud, AI, enterprise) buy the GPUs/servers (10-K; portfolio product pages; transcripts/DCD; both passes). **No portfolio company is evidenced as a systematic server purchaser** (GAP; the only plausible compute-inventory owner is Aptum, managed services/IaaS, with no disclosure of material server buying — raw p2).
3. **The GLXY pattern applies:** the server buyer is the tenant, not the asset owner. Named, evidenced tenant-buyers here:
   - **CoreWeave** — already deploying NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 at Switch EVO AI Factories (CoreWeave PR 2025-07-03). CoreWeave is the direct server account; Switch is the venue.
   - **Oracle** — anchor at Vantage Lighthouse WI (~902 MW critical IT, 2028 target; Data Center Frontier 2025-10-30), with OpenAI named in coverage. Oracle's Lighthouse-scale procurement is the account; Vantage is the venue.
4. **The wedge: powered-shell adjacency + next-phase tenants.**
   - **Vantage Frontier TX:** 1.4 GW, 10 buildings, first building in service 2H 2026, racks **>250kW liquid-cooled**, anchor tenant **UNDISCLOSED** (Vantage/DB PR 2025-08-19). Whoever signs that anchor lease will need liquid-cooled high-density racks matching Supermicro DLC-class specs on a 2H 2026 deployment clock. Monitoring that anchor announcement is the single highest-value trigger in this account.
   - **Vantage NV1** (224 MW, first building 2026) and **Yondr** (400+ MW committed, path >1 GW) are the same play on shorter/parallel clocks.
   - **Switch** markets >2 MW/cabinet densities — any new EVO tenant beyond CoreWeave is a high-density server prospect.
5. **Whom to approach:**
   - Manager-level ecosystem visibility/introductions: DBRG DC investment team — **Jon Mauck** (Senior MD, leads DC investment strategy), **Brent Mayo** (MD, Data Centers), **Kevin Smithen** (CCSO) (leadership page 2026-07; IR PR 2026-06-08).    - Platform level: portfolio CEOs **Sureel Choksi** (Vantage), **Rob Roy** (Switch), **Raul Martynek** (DataBank) — tenant-fit and reference-design conversations, not purchase orders.
   - The actual POs sit with tenants: **CoreWeave** (existing, named), **Oracle** (named anchor), and the undisclosed Frontier TX anchor (watch item).
6. **Context:** pending SoftBank ownership ($16.00/sh; close expected 2H 2026; PR 2025-12-29) frames DBRG as SoftBank's AI-infrastructure scaling vehicle — deal flow through these shells should expand the tenant funnel post-close. Do not treat the deal as closed; it is pending as of 2026-07-27.
