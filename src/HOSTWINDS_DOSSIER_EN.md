## 1. Bottom line

Hostwinds LLC is a privately held Tukwila (Seattle-metro), Washington bare-metal, VPS, cloud and reseller-hosting provider founded in Tulsa, Oklahoma, running its own ASN (AS54290, 303,360 IPv4 addresses) out of three leased colocation sites — Seattle/Tukwila, Dallas and Amsterdam — plus resold colocation capacity in seven further markets. The commercial verdict is **greenfield AI with a purchasing-authority question attached**: there is no GPU product of any kind, the sellable dedicated fleet is roughly 8–19 years old with no Xeon Scalable and no EPYC anywhere in it, but on 2026-04-29 the company was acquired by HostPapa, Inc., so capex authority may already have moved north before any refresh conversation can start. The single strongest piece of evidence is the live public order form at [clients.hostwinds.com/cart.php?gid=12](https://clients.hostwinds.com/cart.php?gid=12), which sells 20 CPU-only SKUs spanning Xeon 5300 Clovertown (2007) through E3-1270 v6 Kaby Lake (2017), with a desktop i7-3930K in the rack and not one Xeon Scalable or EPYC part — a fully priced, verifiable generational gap. The one thing that could kill the deal is the acquisition itself: [HostPapa's 2026-04-29 press release](https://www.hostpapa.com/about-hostpapa/pressroom/2026-04-29-hostpapa-acquires-hostwinds-to-expand-global-hosting-infrastructure/) disclosed no terms, no retention commitment for founder Peter Holden and no capex plan, and [ARIN RDAP for AS54290](https://rdap.arin.net/registry/autnum/54290) was re-registered to HostPapa (Buffalo, NY) on 2026-05-12 — if Hostwinds no longer buys its own hardware, every specification discussion held with Tukwila is wasted.

## 11. Supermicro sales angle

**Classification: greenfield AI — conditional, with purchasing authority possibly relocated.** This is **not** an incumbent-defence account (no OEM is confirmed), **not** a displacement account (no competing vendor is named), and **not** a platform-conversion account in the WebNX sense. 2. **Authority.** **Hostwinds was acquired by HostPapa on 2026-04-29, and ARIN re-registered AS54290 to HostPapa (Buffalo NY) on 2026-05-12.** Capex authority has very likely moved. **Confirm who holds purchasing before discussing a single specification.**

**Do not open with "we know you're running Supermicro."** The research grade on hardware is **unverified**; the only signal is generic IPMI/BMC naming with zero iDRAC or iLO on the domain, and that is an inference. **Turn it into a question.**
