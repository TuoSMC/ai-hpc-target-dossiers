## 1. Bottom line

Sharon AI is a **Nasdaq-listed (SHAZ) Australian neocloud operator wearing a Delaware corporate shell**, and the honest verdict for this team is that it should be escalated to APAC/Australia coverage rather than registered as a Texas lead. The registrable US entity is SharonAI Holdings Inc., incorporated in Delaware with a principal office at 745 Fifth Avenue, New York; the entity that actually buys hardware is **SharonAI Pty Ltd**, which signs the Lenovo SOW and every executive employment agreement, and the entire fleet lives at NEXTDC M3 Melbourne and NEXTDC S3 Sydney, plus GreenSquareDC Sydney and an undisclosed New Zealand facility ([Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2068385/000149315226036377/form10-q.htm); [Form S-1, 2026-07-31](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2068385/000149315226035629/forms-1.htm)). The scale is real and enormous on paper — **~$4 billion of procurement commitment** under a six-year NVIDIA collaboration signed 2026-06-12, up to **40,000 Grace Blackwell GB300 GPUs**, **62,000+ GPUs planned by mid-2027**, ~132 MW of AI Factory capacity of which 116 MW is contracted — but installed capacity today is roughly **2,000 GPUs** and trailing half-year revenue is **$2,225,396** against $2.27 bn of announced contract value.

Commercially this is a **hard displacement, not greenfield, and four separate parties have a commercial interest in keeping the current OEM mix intact.** Lenovo is confirmed on the compute platform: the company's own published node spec lists "24 x 32GB **TruDDR5** 4800MHz RAM" — TruDDR5 is Lenovo's proprietary memory brand — and the 10-Q discloses **$33,094 thousand** of Equipment-as-a-Service / Device-as-a-Service down payments to Lenovo Global Financial Services (Australia & New Zealand) Pty Limited plus a 60-month managed-infrastructure Statement of Work with ~50% paid upfront ([sharonai.com/ai-cloud/](https://sharonai.com/ai-cloud/); [Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2068385/000149315226036377/form10-q.htm)). **World Wide Technology (WWT Australia Pty Ltd) holds $256,186 thousand — $256.2 million — of equipment prepayments** as integrator of record. Cisco is locked in structurally, not merely commercially: Digital Alpha Advisors, which holds an exclusive technology partnership with Cisco, invested up to US$200M and is a shareholder, and the flagship B300 cluster is branded "Australia's first Cisco Secure AI Factory." NVIDIA controls the OEM shortlist through NCP status, DSX reference designs and a revenue-share plus credit-support structure.

**Supermicro's evidence grade in this account is `circumstantial` and must not be upgraded in conversation.** Supermicro appears **exactly once** across the 10-Q, the DEF 14A, three 8-Ks and the 4.6 MB S-1, and it is not as a purchasing relationship — it is one name in a list of OEMs that NVIDIA Cloud Partner status gives the company access to: "OEM Relationships: Facilitated engagement with OEMs such as Cisco, Lenovo, Dell, and Super Micro to support server procurement and integration" ([Form S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2068385/000149315226035629/forms-1.htm)). No purchase order, no prepayment, no commitment, no named agreement, no Supermicro hardware in any published node spec. **Do not tell anyone internally that Supermicro is an incumbent here.**

The one genuine opening is timing and concentration risk. A company consuming hardware as a financed service while carrying **$256.2M prepaid to a single integrator**, against ~$4 bn of committed procurement, on a **70-person** team, has concentrated supply-chain and prepayment exposure sitting on a balance sheet that just raised $900M. A brand-new CFO, **Anuj Goel, starts 2026-08-24** — two weeks after this research date — and will own that risk. The credible pitch is **supply-base diversification and delivery certainty for the April–August 2027 five-phase NEXTDC ramp**, not price and not features. And under Rule 8 this must not be registered as an end-user deal: hardware flows through WWT Australia (integrator), Lenovo Global Financial Services ANZ (financier) and Dicker Data Limited (distributor), so the channel position has to be cleared before any registration attempt.
