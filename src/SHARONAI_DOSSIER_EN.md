## 1. Bottom line

Sharon AI is a **newly public, capital-flooded, Australian-operating neocloud** — SharonAI Holdings Inc. (Nasdaq: **SHAZ**), CIK 0002068385, Delaware-incorporated, New York registered office, trailing half-year revenue of **$2,225,396** against roughly **$2.27 billion of announced contract value** and a **~$4 billion** procurement commitment to NVIDIA, run by **70 employees, board members, advisors and contractors** ([Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2068385/000149315226036377/form10-q.htm); [Form S-1, 2026-07-31](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2068385/000149315226035629/forms-1.htm)). The honest verdict for this team, stated first because it changes what happens next: **this is not a viable Team 1 USA opportunity, and the CRM-clean status is misleading rather than encouraging.** The registrable US entity is a holding shell; the buying entity is **SharonAI Pty Ltd** in Australia; the fleet lives in **NEXTDC M3 Melbourne** and **NEXTDC S3 Sydney** plus GreenSquareDC and New Zealand; the only Texas asset — the 250 MW Ector County JV that presumably produced the T1 designation in the first place — **was sold on 2026-01-13**.

On hardware the picture is equally unambiguous and equally unhelpful. **Lenovo is the confirmed incumbent commercial relationship on the compute platform** — a 60-month managed-infrastructure Statement of Work dated 2025-12-12 with approximately 50% paid upfront, **$33,094 thousand** of Equipment-as-a-Service / Device-as-a-Service down payments, an XBRL member tag naming Lenovo Global Financial Services, and the company's own published node specification listing "24 x 32GB **TruDDR5** 4800MHz RAM", TruDDR5 being Lenovo's proprietary memory brand ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2068385/000149315226036377/form10-q.htm); [sharonai.com/ai-cloud/](https://sharonai.com/ai-cloud/)). **World Wide Technology (WWT Australia Pty Ltd) is the integrator of record**, carrying **$256,186 thousand** — $256.2 million — of equipment prepayments at 2026-06-30. **Cisco is locked in structurally, not merely commercially**: Digital Alpha Advisors, which holds an exclusive technology partnership with Cisco, invested up to US$200M and is a shareholder, and the flagship B300 cluster is branded "Australia's first Cisco Secure AI Factory" ([S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2068385/000149315226035629/forms-1.htm); [Digital Alpha press release](https://sharonai.com/press-releases/sharon-ai-accelerates-enterprise-ai-high-performance-compute-expansion-with-an-investment-from-digital-alpha-of-up-to-us200m-and-strategic-technology-partnership-with-cisco/)).

**Supermicro appears exactly once across the entire evidence set** — the 10-Q, the DEF 14A, three 8-Ks and the 4.6 MB S-1 — and it is not as a purchasing relationship. The single reference sits in a list of benefits derived from NVIDIA Cloud Partner status: "OEM Relationships: Facilitated engagement with OEMs such as Cisco, Lenovo, Dell, and Super Micro to support server procurement and integration" ([S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2068385/000149315226035629/forms-1.htm)). That establishes membership of an approved OEM panel and **nothing more**: no purchase order, no prepayment, no commitment, no named agreement, no Supermicro hardware in any published node specification. **Grade: circumstantial. Do not tell anyone internally that Supermicro is an incumbent here.**

There is one genuine opening, and it is a timing opening rather than a product one. The company consumes hardware as a **financed service** while carrying **$256.2 million prepaid to a single integrator**, against **~$765.1 million** of near-term purchase commitments, a further **~$217.2 million** for 2,048 NVIDIA B300 units, and **~$4 billion** contracted with NVIDIA — all on a 70-person team. A brand-new CFO, **Anuj Goel**, starts **2026-08-24**, two weeks after this research date, and inherits that concentration risk. If this account is worked at all, it should be worked in **October–November 2026**, through the correct geography, on **supply-base diversification and delivery certainty for the April–August 2027 five-phase NEXTDC ramp** — not on price and not on features. **Escalate to APAC/Australia coverage; do not burn one of the five CRM slots on a T1 registration.**

## 10. Cost ceiling

### 10.1 Assumptions — read these first; they are assumptions, not findings

Stated explicitly so they can be challenged:

1. **Realised rate $1.50–$2.50/GPU/hr**, against a **$2.50 list anchor** for H100 NVL from the [2024-12-08 rate card](http://web.archive.org/web/20241208161827/https://sharonai.com/cloud-pricing/). The band assumes 0–40% off list, since the site says "Contact us for volume and term discount" and the company now sells via take-or-pay contracts.
2. **Paid utilisation 60–80%.**
3. **Operating-cost share 35–45% of revenue**, covering colocation, power, cooling, network and support but **NOT** hardware. These are the exact cost categories the 10-Q itself names: "data center costs — comprising colocation facility fees, internet connectivity, and power consumption."
4. **8 GPUs per node**, per the company's own published spec.
5. **8,760 hours per year.**
6. **Payback measured on hardware acquisition cost only**, excluding integration, spares and freight.
7. **No residual value credited.**
8. **No financing cost** — which is conservative in the wrong direction, given the company actually consumes hardware via a Lenovo EaaS/DaaS structure with an embedded finance charge.

**TWO HEALTH WARNINGS.** First, **the price anchor is roughly 20 months stale** — public pricing was withdrawn during 2025 and the current commercial model is bespoke mega-contracts ($373M, $950M, $1.32bn), so **the true realised rate is unknown**. Second, the [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2068385/000149315226036377/form10-q.htm) explicitly flags that "rising operational costs, particularly for power, colocation services, and network infrastructure, are increasing the cost base for GPU cloud providers", **which pushes the operating-cost share toward the upper end of the assumed band and the ceiling toward the lower end**.
