## 9. Supermicro Sales Angle

*Derived honestly from the verified facts above; inferences are labeled as such.*

**Who actually buys servers in this ecosystem:**

1. **Wholesale build-to-suit tenants — not VNET — are the primary server buyers.** VNET's fastest-growing line is wholesale IDC (+77.4% FY2025; +58.1% Q1 2026): it delivers powered shells, cooling, and MW to hyperscale tenants. The 510 MW Greater Beijing order (Q1 2026 PR, 2026-05-26) is from an unnamed "leading internet customer" that media identify as ByteDance for Doubao AI workloads (Bloomberg 2026-03-03 — media-only). Analogy to the GLXY case: the server buyer is the tenant, not the landlord. The wedge here is **tenant adjacency**: the demand signal (517 MW new wholesale orders YTD 2026; 516 MW under construction; 2.48 GW pipeline) tells you where hyperscaler server deployments in Greater Beijing / Ulanqab are heading over 2026-2028.
2. **VNET as a direct buyer** exists mainly in (a) retail IDC / managed hosting (~1,500 managed-hosting customers on VNET-operated equipment — inference from the managed-hosting model, 20-F business description) and (b) its Microsoft cloud operations (VNET operates Azure/M365 in mainland China — 20-F; who owns that hardware is **GAP**). Server/OEM supplier relationships for VNET's halls: **GAP — not disclosed in any source either pass reviewed.** No claim can be made about incumbent vendors.
3. **Scale of the buildout is real and near-term:** capex guidance RMB10-12B (~US$1.4-1.7B) for FY2026; delivery target 450-500 MW; unit capex ~RMB20,000/kW (pass B-only, earnings call). AIDC ("Hyperscale 2.0") and the Ulanqab AI campus are the stated growth themes.

**Which entity to approach, and how (inference, labeled):**

- **Primary target = the wholesale tenants** (the unnamed 510 MW customer and successor phases 2026-2028), not VNET itself. VNET's disclosures are a map of where those tenants are landing capacity.
- **Secondary target = VNET retail/managed-hosting and cloud-ops refresh cycles** — but the buying committee below the interim CEO is a GAP (no CTO/procurement head disclosed), so first-contact discovery is required.
- **Compliance flag (process note, not a researched fact):** VNET and its tenants are PRC-based; any AI-server pursuit must be cleared against US export-control rules before engagement. This dossier makes no claim about what is licensable.
- **Territory note (internal):** HQ is Beijing, PRC — outside Team 1 USA state territory; per cross-team co-op practice, route through the relevant partner team rather than dropping the account on HQ location alone.

**Honest bottom line:** VNET is a landlord signal, not a confirmed server PO. The verified material contains **zero** evidence of VNET's server vendors, GPU counts, or procurement contacts. The actionable intelligence is the MW trajectory, the pending CATL-affiliate ownership change (Q4 2026), and the media-reported ByteDance anchor tenant — all of which point at *tenant-side* server demand in Greater Beijing and Ulanqab.
