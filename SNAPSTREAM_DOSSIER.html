1. Bottom line

SnapStream Media, Inc. is a privately held, Texas-chartered (SOS file 0800059641, charter effective 2001-09-20, status ACTIVE) broadcast-video recording and search company headquartered in a furnished coworking suite at 4201 Main St., Suite 200, Houston, TX 77002-4411 — Suite 200 of The Ion is the Industrious flex-office floor, which means the company has no server room and will never have one; every machine it buys goes to colocation (TX Comptroller franchise-tax record, pulled 2026-08-13 00:29 UTC; SnapStream Master Services Agreement). Commercially this is not a colo-tenant refresh account — it is an appliance-OEM supply-chain account, and that single reframing is the most valuable thing in this file. SnapStream designs, builds, ships and installs its own rackmount servers to third parties: it publishes power tables for nine platform families across at least seven hardware generations (Tuner Node v7, Transcode v5, Master Node v4, Capture v4), it publishes chassis form factors, drive counts and PSU wattages down to the watt, and it sells those systems to the U.S. Navy, DOJ, DOE, DHS and the White House Communications Agency (SnapStream KB, "Power Specifications", last modified 2026-08-04; api.usaspending.gov, 37 awards matched). The single strongest piece of evidence is their own published geometry — 3U / 16 hot-swap bays / dual 800 W redundant / 25.5″ deep and 4U / 24 hot-swap bays / dual 900 W / 26.0″ (660 mm) deep — which is an exact dimensional and PSU fingerprint for the Supermicro SC836 and SC846 chassis families, meaning the fleet is either already Supermicro-based or built on chassis that are dimensionally interchangeable with Supermicro. That must be asked, not asserted. The thing that could kill the deal is the same thing that makes it: they are a ~30-person company with a two-person board and no outside capital, so the consolidation-only first deal is small ($210k–$315k at a 24–36 month payback, §10) — the real prize is becoming the build partner behind an OEM that has a funded five-year federal "SnapStream Modernization" contract with roughly $737k of ceiling still unobligated through 2030-06-30.

14. Supermicro sales angle

Classification: Appliance OEM → build-partner conversion (not a colo-tenant refresh)

The five things that are true and load-bearing

  1. The 2018 baseline is now eight years old — and they publish the numbers themselves, so the comparison is arithmetic, not argument. Their own KB pins current power characteristics to "systems shipped after April 2018" and dates the X710 NIC to 2018. 6 Gb/s Broadcom 9271 controllers, 10 GbE SFP+, 15 TB maximum per node. Every one of those is a spec you can beat by an order of magnitude.
  2. Storage density is the whole consolidation argument. Their largest published node is 15 TB across 24 bays in 4U. A modern 24-bay 4U carries 400 TB+ raw in the same 4U, the same footprint, and better power per terabyte — a >25× density improvement in a business whose product is "instantly search through years of your recordings." Rack reclaim is the immediate quantifiable win: §10 sizes it at $105k/yr freed and $210k–$315k of hardware funded at a 24–36 month payback.

14. Supermicro 銷售切入點

分類：設備原廠（appliance OEM）→ 製造夥伴轉換 （不是 colo 租戶汰換案）

不要以「你們有 19 個機櫃、我們賣伺服器」開場。 他們知道自己有幾個機櫃，而且對現有供應商並無不滿。要用別人都沒注意到的那件事開場：

五件成立且承重的事

  1. 2018 年的基準線已經八年了——而且數字是他們自己公布的，所以這是算術，不是爭論。 其自家 KB 將現行功耗特性錨定於 「systems shipped after April 2018」，並將 X710 網卡定在 2018 年。6 Gb/s Broadcom 9271 控制器、10 GbE SFP+、每節點最高 15 TB。 每一項你都能以數量級勝出。
  2. 儲存密度就是整個整併論述。 他們最大的公開節點是 4U、24 槽、15 TB。今天同樣一台 24 槽 4U，可在相同 4U、相同占地、更佳每 TB 功耗下裝進 400 TB+ 原始容量——對一家產品訴求是 「instantly search through years of your recordings」 的公司而言，這是 >25 倍的密度提升。機櫃釋出是立即可量化的勝點：第 10 節估為每年釋出 $105k、在 24–36 個月回收期下可支應 $210k–$315k 的硬體。
  3. 他們正處在擴張週期中，而且這個週期有節奏。 2021 年 11 月 → 2023 年 4 月 → 2024 年 3 月 → 2025 年 5 月（一天開兩個站）。 間隔 12 至 17 個月。窗口約在 2026 年 7 月重新開啟。 誰在第六個站點被規劃時人在裡面，誰就同時替休士頓、亞特蘭大與紐約寫下標準機櫃套件。
  4. 聯邦端有錢，而 TAA 是資格門檻而非功能賣點。 DISA／WHCA 已動支 $305,476.30，對應 $1,042,344.70 的上限，執行至 2030-06-30，項目名稱字面上就叫 「SNAPSTREAM MODERNIZATION」，並以 NASA SEWP 上的 SBA 保留標交付。Portsmouth Naval Shipyard 的訂閱於 2026-07-14 屆期。 Supermicro 的美國組裝、TAA/BAA 合規產品線是採購資格——而他們既有的經銷商（Enterprise Technology Solutions、Alvarez LLC）本來就在 SEWP 上。
  5. 他們的房東正在蓋下一步需要的機房，而且免費替他們上架。 TRG 目前已支援 50 kW 機櫃、24 MW 擴建動工中、Phase 3 導入 direct-to-chip 液冷，且 remote hands 與跳線全部免費。TRG ＋ Supermicro 的聯合動作——把高密度節點送到一個會免費替一家三十人公司安裝的機房，而這家公司的文件站憑證從五月過期到現在——正好消除他們真正的兩個限制：機櫃空間與人力。
