1. Bottom line

Cybercon, Inc. is a 30-year-old St. Louis dedicated-server, colocation and IP-hosting operator running AS7393 out of Netrality's 210 N Tucker carrier hotel — and it is no longer an independent Midwest host. Australian operator Edge Centres acquired its parent, Hyson International Corp., on 2023-03-29 (EdgeIR; PitchBook), Cybercon then acquired Orion Data Centers of Columbia, Missouri on 2023-06-02 (Baxtel; DataCenterDynamics), and the whole thing now trades publicly as Digital Space — cybercon.com 301-redirects to digitalspace.com, repositioned on "high-performance computing, AI workloads, and enterprise-grade cloud." Commercially this is a platform-conversion target with a financing problem, not an incumbent-defence account: no source anywhere names a server vendor, chassis model or motherboard brand, and the buying authority now sits offshore.

Three findings change the pitch, and all three are primary-record:

  1. They sold their IPv4 to Amazon and now rent the address space they announce. ARIN shows the legacy 66.201.64.0/18 transferred to Amazon.com, Inc. on 2023-03-30 — one day after the Edge Centres deal closed (ARIN RDAP 66.201.64.0), with 49 sub-blocks SWIP'd back under netname AWS-CBCN-LEASEBACK and none of them announced by anyone. Of the 141 IPv4 prefixes AS7393 actually announces today, essentially all are leased RIPE-region space from IPXO, Legaco Networks B.V., Kriek Networks BV and Provizo IP BV (RIPEstat). They converted a one-time capital gain into a permanent operating cost, and address supply — not core count — is now their binding product constraint.
  2. The published catalogue has not moved in at least 23 months, and the silicon in it is 2013-era. Two Wayback captures of /linux-dedicated-servers/ — 2022-05-21 and 2024-04-07 — are byte-identical on prices, CPU strings and storage, and both still carry an unedited "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet" placeholder in the bandwidth block. Zero CPU-generation refreshes observed 2022→2024. No Intel Scalable, no AMD EPYC, ever.
  3. Both Missouri entities are in administrative dissolution, ten days before this dossier's date. Cybercon, Inc. (charter 00512704) received an Administrative Dissolution Notification on 2026-08-07 for failure to file its registration report; parent Hyson International Corp. (charter 00374910) is in the same status (Missouri SOS Business Filings). Signature authority is currently defective.

The wedge is the 2 MW hole. Edge Centres announced EC105 in St. Louis on 2023-11-06 at 2 MW / 28,000 sq ft (EdgeIR) and a stated plan for 20 US regional edge sites in three years — while the public catalogue still sells dual 10-core Xeons at four price points unchanged since 2022, and the "AI dedicated server" is a $99/month CPU blade with TensorFlow preinstalled and no GPU of any kind. The thing that could kill the deal is the money. The flagship SKU supports roughly $5.0k–$7.9k of hardware at a 24-month payback — it does not reach a single modern GPU node at any horizon. Lead with financing structure and memory/GPU allocation, not with a spec sheet, and clear the entity status before anyone signs anything.

10. Cost ceiling

What each server can be worth to Cybercon, and what the same box costs to build. This section answers one question: at what hardware cost does a Cybercon SKU still pay for itself?

10.1 Assumptions — read these first, they are assumptions and not findings

OPERATING-COST DEDUCTION: 45%–65% of gross monthly revenue, midpoint 55%. Ceiling = monthly_price × (1 − opex_share) × N months, so the hardware budget is 35%–55% of revenue. THIS IS AN ASSUMPTION, NOT A RESEARCHED FACT — it is a band selected for this analysis, not something Cybercon disclosed. Treat every ceiling figure below as a modelled estimate.

  • Why the band is set high. The usual industry midpoint for a dedicated server is nearer 40%. It is raised here for one specific, sourced reason: Cybercon sold its own IPv4 and now leases the address space it announces (§8, §11). Every SKU bundles 8 IPv4 addresses, so address rent is a recurring per-server cost that a self-owned-space operator simply does not carry. Add power, cooling, cross-connects, IP transit, remote hands, support, blade-chassis amortisation, and billing/churn allowance, and 45–65% is the defensible range. The direction of this adjustment is evidenced; the exact magnitude is not.
  • What is excluded. No cost of capital or discounting (a 24-month payback at a realistic 10–12% cost of capital is ~8–10% more demanding than the undiscounted figure shown); no sales, licensing or overhead allocation; no residual-value credit at end of life.
  • Payback-window choice. 12 / 18 / 24 months. Note that hardware in this segment is routinely held 4–6 years — this catalogue was still selling 2013-era Xeons in 2024 — so a 24-month payback is a commercial hurdle rate, not an asset-life constraint. Read the 24-month column as "the price at which this SKU is comfortably profitable," not "the price above which it is impossible."
  • A structural caveat on all of it. These ceilings are computed from the 2022/2024 published catalogue, which §9 shows has not moved in at least 23 months, and the current post-rebrand catalogue is unreadable. If Digital Space is now selling at different price points, every figure below moves. The method holds; the inputs need re-reading the moment the live catalogue becomes legible.

10. 成本天花板

每一台伺服器對 Cybercon 值多少，以及同一台機器要多少錢才組得出來。本節回答一個問題：在什麼硬體成本之下，一個 Cybercon SKU 才能自償？

10.1 假設——請先讀這一段；這些是假設，不是查證發現

營運成本扣除：月營收的 45%–65%，中點 55%。 天花板 = 月價 × (1 − 營運成本比) × N 個月，即硬體預算為營收的 35%–55%。這是假設，不是查證發現——是本分析選定的區間，不是 Cybercon 揭露的數字。以下每一個天花板數字都應視為模型估計值。

  • 為何把區間拉高。 專用伺服器的業界常用中點接近 40%。此處調高，是基於一個具體且有來源的理由：Cybercon 已賣掉自有 IPv4，現在承租其對外供裝的位址空間（第 8、11 節）。每一款 SKU 綁 8 個 IPv4 位址，因此位址租金是「自有位址業者根本不會有」的每台經常性成本。再加上電力、冷卻、跨接、IP transit、遠端維運、支援、刀鋒機箱攤提與帳務／流失準備，45–65% 是可辯護的範圍。這項調整的方向有證據支撐；確切幅度沒有。
  • 未納入者。 未計資金成本或折現（在 10–12% 的合理資金成本下，24 個月回收的實際難度比未折現數字高約 8–10%）；未分攤銷售、授權與管銷；未計期末殘值。
  • 回收期選擇。 12／18／24 個月。請注意此區間的硬體通常持有 4–6 年——這份型錄到 2024 年都還在賣 2013 年的 Xeon——因此 24 個月是商業門檻報酬率，不是資產壽命限制。 24 個月那一欄應讀作「在此價格下這個 SKU 舒服地賺錢」，而不是「超過此價格就不可能」。
  • 對全部數字的結構性但書。 這些天花板是由 2022／2024 的公開型錄計算而來，而第 9 節顯示該型錄至少 23 個月未動，且改版後的現行型錄無法讀取。若 Digital Space 現在的價位不同，以下每一個數字都會移動。 方法成立；輸入值必須在現行型錄可讀的那一刻重新讀過。
