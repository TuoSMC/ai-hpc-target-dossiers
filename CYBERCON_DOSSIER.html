1. Bottom line

Cybercon, Inc. — trading since 2026-02-16 as Digital Space (digitalspace.com) — is a St. Louis dedicated-server, colocation and cloud operator running AS7393, owned since 2023-03-29 by Edge Centres Pty of Grafton NSW, Australia, whose founder Jonathan Eaves is the registered agent of record for both Missouri entities (Missouri SOS Business Filings, live session 2026-08-17). Commercially this is an HP-blade displacement account that is financing-gated, not a Supermicro incumbent-defence account — and four primary records taken this pass overturn the working picture. First, the hardware vendor is no longer an open question: their own page advertised "With HP iLo Remote Console customers can self Install OS, reboot servers…" continuously across the Wayback captures of 2012-11-01, 2014-02-08, 2017-10-09 and 2019-09-19, alongside an FAQ describing a blade "powered by six power supplies with three on A power source and three on B" — the six-PSU signature of an HP BladeSystem c7000 enclosure (Wayback 2017-10-09). Second, their landlord holds $3,448,439.60 of unsatisfied judgment against the parent, entered 2026-03-24 in 210 N Tucker Owner, LLC v Hyson International Corp (2422-CC00436), with Date of Satisfaction "not yet on file" on both judgments (Missouri Case.net, read 2026-08-17). Fourth, the CEO's working email is published by their own system — every catalogue and location record carries "created_by": "jon.eaves@edgecentres.com". The wedge sits in the gap between two of their own documents: their 2026-02-16 release commits them to "High-density GPU infrastructure", while their own colocation product publishes a Full Rack at 42 RU and 3.5 kW — roughly two A100 PCIe nodes, and zero 8-GPU HGX nodes. The thing that can kill this deal is not the spec: it is an 8-person parent that has raised $14.7M lifetime, carrying a $3.45M unsatisfied judgment accruing at 9%, with both Missouri entities placed into administrative dissolution on 2026-08-07. Credit and signature authority must be cleared before anything is quoted.

What that means for the pitch:

  1. Do not lead with per-node price. The binding constraint is not the node, it is kW. A Full Rack at 3.5 kW generating $1,249/mo gives $10,492–$16,487 of hardware per rack over 24 months — which is roughly one GPU node per rack. Any pitch that assumes rack-scale density is arithmetically dead on arrival.
  2. Lead with revenue per kW inside a fixed envelope. The honest question is: how many billable GPU-hours fit in 3.5 kW today on a decade-old blade standard, versus 3.5 kW on current-generation nodes? That is a conversation about their P&L, not about a spec sheet, and it is the only version of this pitch that survives their own published numbers.
  3. A facility transition is the only moment a chassis standard changes for free. If the St. Louis suite really is moving (§5), that is the moment — and it is why the qualifying question in §14 asks about the power envelope and the site in the same sentence.
  4. Expect a financing structure with hardware attached, not the reverse. An 8-person parent that has raised $14.7M lifetime and carries $3.45M of unsatisfied judgment at 9% will not fund a GPU hall from cash. §8 must be cleared before terms are discussed.
  5. There is one quotable pricing inconsistency, and it is theirs. The Storage Optimized SKU is $449 on the marketing site and $499 in the actual cart. 10. 成本天花板

每一台伺服器對 Cybercon 值多少，以及同一台機器要多少錢才組得出來。本節回答一個問題：在什麼硬體成本之下，一個 Cybercon SKU 才能自償？

10.1 假設——請先讀這一段；這些是假設，不是查證發現

營運成本扣除：月營收的 45%–65%，中點 55%。 天花板 = 月價 × (1 − 營運成本比) × N 個月，即硬體預算為營收的 35%–55%。這是假設，不是查證發現——是本分析選定的區間，不是 Cybercon 揭露的數字。以下每一個天花板數字都應視為模型估計值。

  • 為何把區間拉高。 專用伺服器的業界常用中點接近 40%。此處調高，是基於一個具體且有來源的理由：Cybercon 已賣掉自有 IPv4，現在承租其對外供裝的位址空間（第 8、11 節）。每一款 SKU 綁 8 個 IPv4 位址，因此位址租金是「自有位址業者根本不會有」的每台經常性成本。再加上電力、冷卻、跨接、IP transit、遠端維運、支援、刀鋒機箱攤提與帳務／流失準備，45–65% 是可辯護的範圍。這項調整的方向有證據支撐；確切幅度沒有。
  • 未納入者。 未計資金成本或折現（在 10–12% 的合理資金成本下，24 個月回收的實際難度比未折現數字高約 8–10%）；未分攤銷售、授權與管銷；未計期末殘值。
  • 回收期選擇。 12／18／24 個月。請注意此區間的硬體通常持有 4–6 年——這份型錄到 2024 年都還在賣 2013 年的 Xeon——因此 24 個月是商業門檻報酬率，不是資產壽命限制。 24 個月那一欄應讀作「在此價格下這個 SKU 舒服地賺錢」，而不是「超過此價格就不可能」。
  • 對全部數字的結構性但書。 這些天花板是由 2022／2024 的公開型錄計算而來，而第 9 節顯示該型錄至少 23 個月未動，且改版後的現行型錄無法讀取。若 Digital Space 現在的價位不同，以下每一個數字都會移動。 方法成立；輸入值必須在現行型錄可讀的那一刻重新讀過。
