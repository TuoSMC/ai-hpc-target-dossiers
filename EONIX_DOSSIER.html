1. Bottom line

Eonix Corporation is a privately held Nevada corporation (entity C24566-2004, incorporated 2004-09-14) trading as ServerHub® — formerly Infinitie Networks — a founder-led dedicated-server, colocation and IPv4-address business running AS62904 (North America) and AS49532 (E.U.), with its self-declared flagship datacenter at phoenixNAP in Phoenix, Arizona — inside Team 1's own territory (PeeringDB net/10140, aka: "dba ServerHub", facility 917; ServerHub press archive, phoenixNAP selection announced 2012-10-01). Commercially this is an incumbent Supermicro account in run-off, not a conversion target and not a growth account: every ServerHub product page from 2015-01-10 through 2026-05-23 carries the company's own sentence "you can reload your Operating System using the Supermicro IPMI tool" (dedicated-servers, 2015-01-10 capture, 2026-05-23 capture), and no Dell, HPE, Lenovo, ASUS, Gigabyte, Tyan or Quanta string appears anywhere on the live site, in the twenty-item press archive, or in any of the seventeen archived catalogue snapshots read for this file.

The account's problem is not vendor preference — it is that they are not currently buying servers because they are not currently selling them. All three product groups on the live WHMCS storefront (Proxmox KVM, Dedicated, Promotion – Dec 2023) return the literal string "Product group does not contain any visible products," and five SKU IDs pulled from the live marketing catalogue (pid=625, 747, 755, 761, 765, spanning dedicated and GPU lines) each redirect to an empty store (my.serverhub.com/store, tested 2026-08-17). The marketing catalogue has been byte-frozen since 2018-08-06; the GPU catalogue has sat on NVIDIA Tesla K80 (Kepler, 2014) at identical prices since at least 2019-07-22.

What is alive is the IP address business. AS62904 announces 663 IPv4 prefixes / 451,840 addresses today (RIPEstat, window 2026-08-03→2026-08-17), against ~575,488 IPv4 addresses held in thirteen direct ARIN parent allocations (ARIN Org EONIX nets). A 110-prefix random RDAP sample resolved 38% of most-specific blocks to third-party organisations, and the full bgp.tools description census names IPXO LLC (an IPv4 lease marketplace), Buyproxies.org, GHOSTnet GmbH (10 prefixes), Direct Inbox Delivery, Delivery Matrix LLC and a dozen more marketing/ESP/proxy operators. One ARIN net is literally named NET240-LEASE. Answer to the brief's core question — servers or IP leasing: IP leasing. Do not size this account off the prefix count.

The honest opening is therefore consolidation-for-colo-cost, sold through the phoenixNAP landlord relationship — not a refresh pitch, not a GPU pitch, and certainly not a displacement pitch against a vendor that has been theirs for eleven years.

10. Cost ceiling

What each server can be worth to Eonix, and what the same box would cost to build today. This section answers one question: at what hardware cost does an Eonix SKU still pay for itself?

10.1 Assumptions — read these first, they are assumptions and not findings

OPERATING-COST DEDUCTION: a band of 45%–70% of gross monthly rent. Ceiling = monthly_price × (1 − opex) × N months. THIS IS AN ASSUMPTION, NOT A RESEARCHED FACT. Eonix publishes no cost data of any kind.

  • What the band covers: phoenixNAP space and power, blended transit (their actual upstreams are Cogent AS174, Arelion AS1299, Hurricane Electric AS6939, Wowrack AS23033 and NYI AS11403 — see §11), IPv4 carrying cost, remote hands, 24×7 support labour, WHMCS/cPanel/Windows licensing, payment processing, and an allowance for unsold or idle inventory and churn.
  • Why 45%–70% and not a single number. 45% is a lean, high-utilisation host. 70% is a host with soft utilisation. Given an empty storefront, 70% is the more defensible end here, and the 70% column should be treated as the operative one.
  • Unlike an owner-operator, Eonix has no low-cost site. They own no building anywhere (§5) — every one of the eleven US facilities and both EU sites is leased colocation. There is no Ogden-equivalent where opex sits at the low end, which is precisely why the high end of the band is the realistic one.
  • What is excluded. No cost of capital or discounting (a 24-month payback at a realistic 10–12% cost of capital is ~8–10% more demanding than shown); no sales, support or overhead allocation; no residual-value credit at end of life, which for used-Xeon and storage chassis is non-trivial and would raise the effective ceiling.
  • The list prices themselves are stale and unbuyable. The flagship price has been static since 2018-08-06 while every input cost rose, and Preferred Member / Reseller discounts run to 30% (reseller · preferred-members). Every ceiling below is therefore an upper bound on an upper bound.

  • Not a volume refresh. Their storefront sells nothing (all three product groups empty, five tested SKU IDs dead). There is no revenue growth to fund a fleet purchase.

  • Not a competitive displacement. They are already yours. There is no Dell or HPE to knock out.

What the wedge IS

Consolidation-for-colo-cost, sold to the landlord relationship.

Eonix is a long-standing, self-integrating, single-vendor Supermicro customer running a fleet whose newest generation is Skylake-SP (2017) and whose volume tier is Ivy Bridge-EP E5 v2 (2013), in leased cabinets at phoenixNAP, with a ~575k-address IPv4 portfolio that is now more valuable and more liquid than the metal (§11). They are carrying eight-to-thirteen-year-old silicon at 2026 Phoenix colo power rates, for a book of business their own storefront no longer takes orders for.

The proposition is not "buy more servers." It is: collapse the estate and stop paying phoenixNAP for the difference.

One current dual-socket Supermicro 2U node — a SYS-621P-TRT or SYS-221H-TNR class system — carries the core count of four to six of their dual Gold 6148 boxes (engineering estimate, labelled as such), and far more than that against the E5 v2 tier, in the same 2U at a fraction of the watts. Consolidating a single row frees rack units, frees kW, and cuts the colo invoice without touching a single customer's IP address — which is the asset they actually care about protecting. It also preserves their entire operating model: Supermicro IPMI, the same tooling, the same hand-build-and-burn-in workflow, the same technicians.

Frame it as an OpEx reduction with a hardware line item, sized at the §10.2 ceiling ($5,400–$9,900 per node at 18-month payback), quoted for one pilot rack, and evidence-backed with a $/kW-delivered-core comparison against their own published Gold 6148 and E5-2660v2 configurations. Bring the power math, not a product catalogue.

Where the deal actually gets found: phoenixNAP. They are a tenant, not an owner, at every one of their thirteen facilities. Their landlord's power-density economics — not their own roadmap — will force this decision. Work facility 917 as a co-sell channel; that team holds a renewal calendar you cannot see.

Secondary wedge, opportunistic: Brancela has done a 1,200-node, three-week, three-site burst before. If a single large customer lands, this account goes from zero to a multi-hundred-node emergency procurement overnight. Keep it warm and keep the relationship at CEO level, because that is where that call would come from and there is no procurement function to route it through.

The one qualifying question for first contact

"You've been running Supermicro IPMI on this fleet since at least 2015 — the Gold 6148 and E5 v2 tiers you list today. What is phoenixNAP charging you per kW now versus when you signed, and how many cabinets would you get back if we collapsed six of those Gold 6148 nodes into one 621P?"

Ask this and very little else on the first call. It proves you read their catalogue; it names their vendor back to them without asking, which is the correct posture for an eleven-year incumbency; it puts the pain where the pain actually is — the colo invoice, not the roadmap; and it asks for a number they know and you do not, which is the only kind of question that gets a three-person company to call back.

10. 成本天花板

每一台伺服器對 Eonix 值多少錢，以及同一台機器今天要花多少錢組出來。本節只回答一個問題：在什麼硬體成本之下，Eonix 的 SKU 還能回本？

10.1 假設——請先讀這一段；這些是假設，不是查證發現

營運成本扣除：以月租金的 45%–70% 為區間。 天花板 = 月租 × (1 − 營運成本比) × N 個月。這是假設，不是查證得到的事實。 Eonix 未公布任何形式的成本資料。

  • 這個區間涵蓋什麼： phoenixNAP 的空間與電力、混合中繼流量（實際上游為 Cogent AS174、Arelion AS1299、Hurricane Electric AS6939、Wowrack AS23033 與 NYI AS11403——見第 11 節）、IPv4 持有成本、遠端維運、24×7 支援人力、WHMCS／cPanel／Windows 授權、金流處理，以及未售出或閒置庫存與流失的準備。
  • 為何是 45%–70% 而非單一數字。 45% 對應精實、高使用率的業者；70% 對應使用率疲弱的業者。在商店已空的前提下，70% 才是此處較可辯護的一端，因此 70% 那一欄應視為實務值。
  • 與自有機房業者不同，Eonix 沒有任何低成本站點。 他們在任何地方都沒有自有建物（第 5 節）——十一個美國設施與兩個歐盟站點全部是租用主機代管。這裡不存在「Ogden 等價」的低成本端，這正是區間高端才是現實值的原因。
  • 未計入之項目： 未計資金成本或折現（在合理的 10–12% 資金成本下，24 個月回本比表列數字再嚴苛約 8–10%）；未分攤業務、支援或管銷；未計期末殘值——而對二手 Xeon 與儲存機殼而言殘值並不小，計入會提高實際天花板。
  • 牌價本身既過時又買不到。 旗艦價自 2018-08-06 起原地不動，其間所有投入成本都上漲；且 Preferred Member／Reseller 折扣可達 30%（reseller · preferred-members）。因此以下每一個天花板，都是「上限之上的上限」。

14. Supermicro 銷售切入點

分類：停業收縮中的既有客戶 → 以整併換取機房成本

這不是轉換案，也不是更新換代案。 Supermicro 的存在已由客戶自家公開頁面連續十一年確認——Supermicro IPMI tool 出現在自 2015-01-10 至 2026-05-23 每一份存檔快照的每一個產品頁上，且任何競品系統廠從未出現。廠商這個問題已經有答案了。不要再問，也不要推銷替換。

這個楔子「不是」什麼

  • 不是 GPU／AI 訴求。 他們從未賣過比 Tesla K80 更新的東西，母公司定位中也沒有任何 AI 語彙。以當世代 GPU 平台開場，會讓對方認定你沒讀過他們的網站。
  • 不是量產更新換代。 他們的商店賣不出東西（三個產品群組全空，五個實測 SKU 代號全死）。沒有任何營收成長能支撐機隊採購。
  • 不是新增產能。 他們已從宣稱的 13 個站點縮到 7 個線上 looking glass，AS30693 也在 2026 年 1 月退役。他們在收縮。
  • 不是競品替換。 他們早就是你們的客戶。這裡沒有 Dell 或 HPE 可以打掉。

這個楔子「是」什麼

以整併換取機房成本，並透過房東關係去談。

Eonix 是一家長期、自行組裝、單一廠商的 Supermicro 客戶，其機隊最新世代是 Skylake-SP（2017）、主力層是 Ivy Bridge-EP E5 v2（2013），全部放在 phoenixNAP 的租用機櫃裡，同時手握一個如今比機器更值錢也更具流動性的約 575k 位址 IPv4 資產組合（第 11 節）。他們正以 2026 年的 Phoenix 機房電價，扛著八到十三年前的矽晶，服務一個連自家商店都已經不再接單的客戶簿。

訴求不是「多買幾台伺服器」，而是：把機隊收攏起來，別再為那個差額付錢給 phoenixNAP。

一台當世代雙路 Supermicro 2U 節點——SYS-621P-TRT 或 SYS-221H-TNR 等級——其核心數相當於他們四到六台雙路 Gold 6148（工程估計值，並如此標示），對上 E5 v2 主力層則遠不止；同樣是 2U，耗電只是零頭。整併一排機櫃可釋出機架單位、釋出 kW、砍掉機房帳單，而且完全不必動到任何一個客戶的 IP 位址——那才是他們真正在意要保住的資產。 同時，這也完整保留他們的營運模式：Supermicro IPMI、同一套工具、同一套自行組裝與 burn-in 流程、同一批技術人員。

請把它包裝成「一筆帶硬體品項的營運費用削減案」，規模抓 10.2 節的天花板（18 個月回本下每節點 $5,400–$9,900），報價只報一個試行機櫃，並以對照其自家公布之 Gold 6148 與 E5-2660v2 組態的 $／kW 交付核心 數據為佐證。帶電力數字去，不要帶產品型錄去。

這筆生意實際上會在哪裡被找到：phoenixNAP。 他們在十三個設施裡全部是租戶，不是屋主。逼出這個決策的會是房東的電力密度經濟學，不是他們自己的路線圖。 請把設施 917 當成共同銷售管道經營；那邊的團隊握有你看不到的續約時程。

次要楔子，機會性質： Brancela 做過1,200 台、三週、三個站點的爆量部署。若有單一大客戶落地，本帳戶會在一夜之間從零變成數百台的緊急採購。保持溫度，並把關係維持在執行長層級——因為那通電話會從那裡打來，而且中間沒有採購部門可以轉。

首次接觸的唯一資格問題

「你們這批機器從 2015 年起就在跑 Supermicro IPMI——就是你們現在還掛在架上的 Gold 6148 和 E5 v2 那兩層。phoenixNAP 現在跟你們收的每 kW 價格，比當初簽約時貴多少？如果把六台 Gold 6148 收成一台 621P，你們可以拿回幾個機櫃？」

第一通電話問這一句，其他少講。它證明你讀過他們的型錄；它把他們的廠商名字說回去給他們聽而不是去問——這才是面對十一年既有關係應有的姿態；它把痛點放在真正會痛的地方——機房帳單，不是路線圖；而且它問的是一個他們知道、你不知道的數字，那是唯一能讓一家三人公司回電的問法。
