1. Bottom line

tzulo, inc. is an Illinois corporation, incorporated 25 February 2003, owned and run by one named person — Gurpreet Singh Khanna — selling dedicated servers, colocation and IP transit out of a cage inside CoreSite CH1, 427 S LaSalle Street, Chicago, with a second, never-named "private datacenter location in downtown Los Angeles", running AS11878 with 197 announced IPv4 prefixes = 116,224 addresses (RIPEstat, verified 2026-08-17). Commercially this is not a vendor-displacement problem and not a new-logo problem — it is an eleven-year re-engagement problem. tzulo's own announcement of Friday 18 September 2015 launched a "New 1U Supermicro Intel x3470 2.93ghz" dedicated line at $75/month (tzulo announcements, ID 8–15); they state "We own all of our servers" and "We do not resell anyone else's service" (company page, 2009 Wayback capture). Since that 2015 announcement the catalogue has not moved: the dedicated.html served on 2026-03-08 is byte-identical to the page served on 2020-06-09, the CPU list and price list have been static for 10 years and 9 months, both cabinet colocation SKUs and the entire cloud line read SOLD OUT, and the WHMCS store behind every marketing link resolves to an empty cart. The constraint that decides the deal is watts, not floor space: their published cabinet is 20A @ 208V = 3.3 kW across 47U, which a rack of 2010–2012 dual-socket Xeons exhausts at roughly 20 machines — so the only shape of deal their economics carries is performance-per-watt consolidation inside a fixed 3.3 kW envelope, and GPU/AI is not a near-term conversation at that power level. The timing is unusually good and it is datable: IP Transit product rebuilt between Dec 2025 and Apr 2026, transit mix rewritten by Jun 2026, a new diagnostics platform cut 7 Apr 2026, ARIN POCs revalidated Dec 2025 and three fresh /24s allocated Dec 2025 / May 2026 / Jul 2026 — every part of this business has been touched in the last nine months except the servers. The two things that can still kill the deal are unseen: the Illinois UCC index was never reached (§8), so lien position is UNKNOWN not clean, and no CRM/channel check is recorded in this research pass (§14), so the distributor position must be cleared before anyone dials.

14. Supermicro 銷售切入點

分類：沉睡既有客戶 → 每瓦效能整併之重新啟動

這不是新客戶開發案，也不是既有供應商替換案。 tzulo 自家 2015-09-18 的公告推出的正是 "New 1U Supermicro Intel x3470 2.93ghz" 產品線（公告）。若以「他們從沒買過 Supermicro」的姿態開場，等於浪費了本檔最強的事實。

四項事實構成論述，其中三項出自 tzulo 自己的用語。

  1. 他們已經是 Supermicro 買家。 依其 2015-09-18 自家公告。這是重新啟動，不是替換。
  2. 他們完全自有機隊，而且白紙黑字寫著 — "We own all of our servers"、"We do not resell anyone else's service"（2009 年 company 頁）。每一塊更新預算都由他們自己決定：沒有租賃公司、沒有 OEM 綁定、沒有通路要打。
  3. 他們在毛利最好的地方已經沒有可售容量。 兩種機櫃方案最遲自 2018 年起即標示 SOLD OUT，cloud 也是 SOLD OUT。他們 2015 年的公告說明了原因："we were sold out in a matter of months due to our low pricing." 他們有服務不了的需求。
  4. 限制是瓦數，不是樓地板。 公開規格：20A @ 208V ＝ 47U 共 3.3 kW。一櫃 2010–2012 年的雙路 Xeon，約 20 台就把這個上限吃滿。其型錄矽晶為 Westmere、Sandy Bridge 與 Ivy Bridge。

合起來看：tzulo 不需要在 CoreSite CH1 要更多空間，也負擔不起在那裡要更多電。它需要的是在固定 3.3 kW 範圍內取得更多可售單位。 這是一筆每瓦效能整併的生意，也是其經濟結構唯一撐得起的成交形態（10.4 節）。以少量當世代單路 1U 節點，以一抵多地取代雙路 L5630 與雙路 E5-2620，就能把同一個機櫃、同一個安培數轉換成他們真正賣得掉的庫存——並把價格天花板從一份自 2015-11-25 起未動過的價目表上頂起來。

而且時機罕見地好。 他們已經在更新之中（9.2 節）：IP Transit 產品於 2025 年 12 月至 2026 年 4 月間重建；transit 組合於 2026 年 6 月前改寫為 NetActuate／Astound／Zayo／Liberty Global／PCCW；全新診斷平台於 2026 年 4 月 7 日上線；ARIN POC 於 2025 年 12 月重新驗證；2025 年 12 月、2026 年 5 月與 7 月各配發一個新 /24。這門生意過去九個月內每一個環節都動過了，唯獨伺服器沒動。 有人決定要重新投資了。伺服器線就是清單上的下一項，而還沒有人去賣給他們。

不要用 GPU 或 AI 開場。 在每櫃 3.3 kW 的條件下那不是近期成交的題目，提了就等於證明你沒讀過他們的規格頁（第 7 節）。
